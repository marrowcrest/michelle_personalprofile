CASE STUDY 01 EQUESTRIAN TRAINING PROGRAM · MARSHALL, VIRGINIA (US)

Forecast Farms: End-to-End Repositioning, GHL Booking Architecture & Living Content System

GoHighLevel CRM 2-Step Booking Flow SMS & Review Automation GitHub Demo → GHL Deploy SKILL.md Brand Engine
01 / THE BOTTLENECK & AUDIT

Forecast Farms (a family-owned Hunter, Jumper, and Equitation program in Virginia) needed to reposition away from a "beginner children's lesson barn" toward committed junior/adult riders and horse owners—while diagnosing a drop in lesson bookings.

  • Social-to-Web Leak: Audit revealed bio links pointed to a generic homepage instead of a booking intake page, and legacy ecpro links created friction.
  • Positioning Drift: Copy used casual "intro lesson" and "child" phrasing instead of serious equestrian training terminology.
  • Unstructured Follow-Up: No automated pipeline connecting initial inquiry, owner SMS response, and Google review capture.
02 / THE SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE
  • GHL Qualification-First Booking Flow: Retired the legacy booking link. Built a 2-step system: prospects complete an Evaluation Lesson Form first, triggering an automated booking link and routing 2-way SMS directly to the owner's GHL mobile app.
  • Non-Destructive Web Copy Overhaul: Preserved the core visual layout in GitHub (forecast-farms-website) and deployed on GHL—re-engineering copy section-by-section around "Training built around horse and rider" (Rider Training, Horse Training, Horse + Rider Development) in brand purple (#7E2B80).
  • Codified `SKILL.md` Content Engine: Built a version-controlled brand system enforcing "Evaluation lesson" and "rider (ages 7+)" terminology, zero emojis/hashtags, strict 60–120 word IG vs. FB split rules, and a 6-day weekly TOFU→BOFU cadence.
03 / QA & MEASURABLE PROOF
  • Pre-Deploy Verification: Every social post and web section passes a 3-point QA gate (word count, terminology compliance, and theme-bucket rotation so no two adjacent weeks repeat a bucket).
  • Reputation & SMS Loop: Configured online booking, two-way GHL mobile texting, and automated Google review requests upon lesson completion.
  • Client Validation: Approved by the owner for public Upwork portfolio showcase; ongoing retention across both web systems and weekly content engineering.
✓ VERIFIED OUTCOME: Unified GHL Web + Evaluation Form + Two-Way SMS + 10-Column Content Master System

Domain-Isolated Outbound, Rapid Mockup Conversion & Partner Lead-Gen Architecture

Deliverability Architecture Instantly Verification Rapid Homepage Mockups Editorial Marketplace UX Lead Qualification Rules
01 / THE BOTTLENECK & AUDIT

B2B service teams and marketplace ventures often burn their primary domain reputation by sending cold outbound directly from their main CRM—or lose prospects by pitching generic text emails or unsolicited links that trigger spam filters.

  • Domain Reputation Risk: Sending cold outreach from the primary production GHL domain risks blacklisting transactional client emails.

02 / THE SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE
  • Strict Domain Isolation Protocol: Architected a clean separation between production CRM/web domains and dedicated outbound sending domains, paired with automated lead verification and controlled daily inbox throttling (10 emails/inbox/day via Instantly).
  • Algorithmic Lead Qualification Filter: Built strict targeting rules to exclude agency-managed or subscription-locked websites, focusing solely on high-fit independent operators in US (Minnesota, DFW) and Australian markets.
  • Value-First Mockup & Editorial Funnel UX: Combined custom homepage mockup hooks (and permission-based 2-minute Loom teardown triggers) with editorial partner-matching UX (The Covetry model for US kitchen remodelers).
03 / QA & MEASURABLE PROOF
  • Zero Primary-Domain Contamination: Outbound infrastructure operates 100% independently of client-facing GHL transactional messaging.
  • Repeatable Daily Pipeline Engine: Automated daily lead-sourcing and qualification criteria paired with rapid prototype generation to turn cold prospects into warm technical consultations.
