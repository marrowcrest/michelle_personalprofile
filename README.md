## About Me

I'm **Mich Candelaria**, a GoHighLevel CRM and automation specialist. I help service businesses stop losing leads between the phone call, the inquiry form and the calendar.

Before marketing systems, I trained in **structural and civil engineering**, writing calculation reports in ETABS, Mathcad and Excel. In engineering you never guess where a load goes, and I build CRMs the same way. Every lead has a traced path, and every automation is tested before a real customer touches it.

Based in the Philippines (UTC+8), I work async and in daytime overlap with clients in the US, Australia and the UK/EU.
